ABOUT MAGIC U!

Discover the story, mission, and creative team behind Magic U!

Our Story

MAGIC U! was created as a fantasy action role-playing game that brings a fresh and inclusive approach to the RPG genre. The concept combines magical adventure, dramatic action, whimsy, and glamour.

Our team wanted to create a game where a wide range of players could feel represented while exploring a magical universe, developing their character, and taking part in exciting battles and quests.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a fantasy action RPG that places diversity, creativity, and player expression at the centre of the experience.

Through memorable characters, magical environments, action-based gameplay, and inclusive storytelling, MAGIC U! hopes to offer players a world where everyone can feel seen and become the hero of their own adventure.
